## What is Kenshi?

There is no prescribed heroic journey. Start with an individual or grow a squad,
choose your own goals, and find a way to live among competing factions,
dangerous wildlife, and settlements that have concerns beyond your character.
Travel, trade, craft, steal, or build an outpost: the direction is yours.

Progress comes through training and experience rather than a world that scales
down to meet you. Wounds, hunger, captivity, and lost limbs can change a squad's
plans. Survival depends as much on preparation and retreat as on winning a fight.

## From playing to modding

The Forgotten Construction Set (FCS) exposes the data that connects characters,
dialogue, factions, equipment, and much of the world. The in-game World Editor
handles another part of the picture: placing and arranging buildings and scenery.
Start small, understand the references, and keep a separate mod for experiments.

## A living field manual

This site focuses on practical modding knowledge, not a complete encyclopedia of
every item or town in the game. Compare examples, share corrections, and record
what actually works. Guide comments use GitHub Discussions; a GitHub account is
needed to post.

### Help improve the manual

Found an unclear step, a missing detail, or a better way to explain a tool?
Contributions can be as small as a correction or as substantial as a new guide.
Share what you tested, preserve source credits, and include relevant screenshots
when you have permission to reuse them. Visit the
[project Wiki](https://github.com/Atlasroar/Kenshi-Modding-Wiki/wiki) for
contribution guidelines and the website's maintainer documentation.

Work on a test mod and back up saves before experimenting. Older-source warnings
and work-in-progress notices remain important: publication is not a guarantee
that every instruction has been retested in the current game.
