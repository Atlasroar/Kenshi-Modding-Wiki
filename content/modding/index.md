## Forgotten Construction Set

- [[modding/fcs/complete-guide|The FCS - A Complete Guide]] covers the interface,
  data management, searches, and mod loading.

## Dialogue

- [[modding/dialogue/conditions-and-effects|Dialogue Condition & Effects]]
  catalogs talker enums, checks, and effects.
- [[modding/dialogue/find-specific-dialogue|How to Find Specific Dialogue]]
  explains text searches and StringIDs. **Work in progress.**

  describes alternating interjectors.

## World and buildings

- [[modding/world/building-collision-navmesh|Buildings Collision & Navmesh]]
  covers colliders and navigation meshes. **Work in progress.**
- [[modding/world/in-game-editor|In Game Editor]] covers towns, buildings,
  interiors, and placement.
- [[modding/world/fast-healing-beds|Fast Healing Beds]] is a short tip about the
  production multiplier.
- [[modding/world/public-npc-beds|Sleeping On Npc Beds]] covers public bed access.
- [[modding/world/kill-npcs-at-game-start|Killing Npcs at Game Start]] retains
  Cattrina's source method.
- [[modding/world/vendor-lists|Vendor Lists]] covers shop inventories and
  blueprints. **Work in progress.**

## Characters and races

- [[modding/characters/creating-a-race|Creating a New Race Guide]] covers meshes,
  skeletons, and skin textures. **Work in progress.**
- [[modding/characters/invisible-characters|Invisible Characters Guide]]
  discusses invisible meshes and limitations.
- [[modding/characters/name-databases|Name Database Guide]] covers names and
  word swaps.

## Assets and animations

- [[modding/assets/armor-color-maps|Color Map for Armors Guide]] covers color
  channels and includes a glossary of color-map terminology.

  multi-character seating and animations, including untested ideas.

## Save editing

- [[modding/save-editing/claim-town-ownership|Claim Town Ownership]] preserves
