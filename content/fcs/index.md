The Forgotten Construction Set is the starting point for editing Kenshi's game
data. Begin with the complete guide, then follow a focused workflow.

## Start here

- [[modding/fcs/complete-guide|The FCS - A Complete Guide]] covers launching the
  editor, objects and columns, searching and bulk editing, load order, merging,
  cleanup, Steam uploads, and all 17 documented error types in one page.

The guide includes 30 source screenshots and retains its original migration
notices. Source attribution and the Wiki-derived text license are on
[[credits#using-the-fcs-text-license|Credits]].

## Focused workflows

- [[modding/world/fast-healing-beds|Fast Healing Beds]] is a short example of
  changing a bed's production multiplier.
- [[modding/fcs/tech-overhaul-notes|Tech Overhaul Notes]] preserves development
  notes about research templates. It is not a complete tutorial.
