These guides build on knowledge shared by the Kenshi modding community.
This directory records the credits actually present in the source material,
including the difference between an author, a contributor, an image creator,
and a recommended reference.

The initial 17 guides came from the owner's Kenshi Modding Wiki collection.
Each guide retains its source and migration notice. **Author not recorded**
means the imported note did not identify an author; it is not an attribution
to this website or its maintainers.

## Using the FCS: text license

The [Kenshi Wiki article](https://kenshi.fandom.com/wiki/Using_the_FCS),
[revision 92817](https://kenshi.fandom.com/wiki/Using_the_FCS?oldid=92817),
is credited to its contributors through the
[article history](https://kenshi.fandom.com/wiki/Using_the_FCS?action=history).
The Wiki identifies `Using the FCS.pdf`, supplied with Kenshi version 1.0.57,
as its underlying manual.

The Wiki text is available under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
Wiki-derived text and its adaptations on this website remain available under
that license. The article's interface, data-management, mod-management, and
error-reference material is consolidated into
[[modding/fcs/complete-guide|The FCS - A Complete Guide]].
The adaptations condense and rewrite the source, add selection/dependency safety
notes and copyable search examples, transcribe Cattrina's annotated upload
checklist, and consolidate external references onto this page. Thirty original
screenshots are restored in their relevant sections, with local files and
meaningful alt text. This consolidation preserves the original compilation's
other source notices; it does not assign the Wiki text license to unrelated material.
Separately credited sources and images are not automatically relicensed.

The screenshot reuse permission and individual source files are recorded below,
separately from the text license.

## FCS tools and references

- The source article recommends [Shidan's Save Editing 101](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1370334383),
  [Shidan's game/FCS mechanics notes](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2570870493),
  [Boron's FCS and Dialogue guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1444279946),
  and [Canin's How to Find and Edit Mods](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1608980474).
  These are recommended references, not authorship credits for the Wiki article.
- The FCS guide references [Regexr](https://regexr.com/) as a regular-expression
  reference and tester. This is a tool reference, not an author credit.
- The building collision guide references a
  [Reddit example of complex navmesh work](https://www.reddit.com/r/Kenshi/comments/upmm4q/modular_fortresses_for_lita_done/).
  The imported note does not record an individual author for that example.
