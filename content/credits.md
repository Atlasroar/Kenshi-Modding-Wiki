These guides build on knowledge shared by the Kenshi modding community.
This directory records the credits actually present in the source material,
including the difference between an author, a contributor, an image creator,
and a recommended reference.

The initial 17 guides came from the owner's Kenshi Modding Wiki collection.
Each guide retains its source and migration notice. **Author not recorded**
means the imported note did not identify an author; it is not an attribution
to this website or its maintainers.

## Using the FCS: text license

The [Kenshi Wiki article](https://kenshi.fandom.com/wiki/Using_the_FCS),
[revision 92817](https://kenshi.fandom.com/wiki/Using_the_FCS?oldid=92817),
is credited to its contributors through the
[article history](https://kenshi.fandom.com/wiki/Using_the_FCS?action=history).
The Wiki identifies `Using the FCS.pdf`, supplied with Kenshi version 1.0.57,
as its underlying manual.

The Wiki text is available under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
Wiki-derived text and its adaptations on this website remain available under
that license. Website changes include formatting, internal navigation, and
consolidation of external references onto this page. Separately credited
sources and images are not automatically relicensed.

Fandom's [licensing guidance](https://community.fandom.com/wiki/Help:Licensing)
explicitly distinguishes text from uploaded images. The article's screenshots
need their own reuse basis; the text license alone does not establish one.
